What we would change first

Three moves, in order. Everything else in this review is a refinement of them.

Move one

Make sign-in look like sign-in

An email panel first, the persona picker beneath it as a labelled demo shortcut. A board member should recognise the screen in one second.

Move two

Put registration on the event card

One primary action — Register — with places remaining beside it. Saving becomes a secondary link, so the two stop competing.

Move three

Register the household, not the person

Head count, contact details once, reminders on by default. The family field already drafted in the create-member form becomes the unit that registers.
