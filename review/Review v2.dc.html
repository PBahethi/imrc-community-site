The features arrived. The feedback that tells a member they worked did not.

Twenty-two commits added household registration, event requests, a four-day convention workflow, QR check-ins, participation badges, a help network and a locality map. Nine of these are real advances on the last review. What is missing is the layer that makes them legible: the help button exists on three old screens and none of the seven new modules, and the new stylesheets contain no motion at all.

Credit where it is due

Registration is now a real record with attendees, validated and versioned in event-actions-core.js. Requests give an attendee a reason to speak before the day. The convention has genuine venue, airport and coach data with sources. Check-ins and badges are private by construction — no score, no leaderboard. The calendars/EV2028.ics file the last review flagged as missing now exists. The honesty of the copy has survived the growth.

Tooltips and contextual help

The pattern you already built is the right one — app.js’s tip() renders a labelled button with aria-describedby, opens on hover, focus and tap, and closes on Escape. It is called three times in the entire product: people search, reading a relationship, and event dates. Every new control ships without it.

Motion

Search the repository for transition or @keyframes and the whole product returns five: the route-change fade on main, the button hover lift, the event image zoom, the dashboard event-card border, and one poll bar. event-actions.css, convention.css, convention-experience.css, help.css, recognition.css and locality-map.css contain none. So registering, checking in, unlocking a badge and saving logistics all happen with no visible change in state — and because each one re-renders its panel with innerHTML, there is no old state left to animate from.
