IF LEADERSHIP WANTS THE INITIATIVE WORDS KEPT

Then make them initiatives, not filters

The strip borrows its shape from a federation site where those words are standing programmes with their own pages. If IMRC wants that masthead, the words should lead to the initiative — its purpose, its people, its next gathering — and the convention should appear there as one item, rather than the word being a shortcut into a session filter.
