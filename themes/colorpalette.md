# IMRC visual themes

The application uses named color roles instead of assigning meaning by a palette’s position. `theme.js` is the source of truth. Action text is white in every theme; each action/text pairing meets WCAG AA contrast for normal text.

| Theme | Surface | Ink | Action | Action text | Quiet surface | Accent | Assigned chapters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Indian Flag | `#f8f9fc` | `#172750` | `#153eaa` | `#ffffff` | `#edf2ff` | `#ff9933` | IMRC National / default |
| Jaisalmer | `#fff8e9` | `#54210b` | `#9a4f07` | `#ffffff` | `#fff3dc` | `#f2a51a` | Southeast, Canada West |
| Jodhpur | `#f4f7ff` | `#17366b` | `#1f5496` | `#ffffff` | `#e6edff` | `#6da4ff` | Northeast, Mid-Atlantic |
| Udaipur | `#fffceb` | `#3d3b3b` | `#4a4442` | `#ffffff` | `#f4eee0` | `#d2cac5` | Midwest |
| Jaipur | `#fff5f0` | `#4b1d14` | `#762817` | `#ffffff` | `#fff0e9` | `#c9775e` | Southwest |
| Peacock | `#f2fbfa` | `#123744` | `#174a70` | `#ffffff` | `#e4f4f2` | `#3e93aa` | West Coast, Canada East & Central |

Members receive their chapter theme after sign-in. An explicit choice in the theme picker persists across reloads until “chapter theme” is selected again.
